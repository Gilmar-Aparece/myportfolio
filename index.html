About Me

My Journey

I am a detail-oriented WordPress Developer and 3D enthusiast with a passion for building secure, high-performance digital experiences. My journey is rooted in the technical challenge of creating seamless web environments—from architecting complex student capstone projects with 100% uptime to integrating AI-powered security dashboards.

I believe that a website is a living ecosystem where performance meets security. Whether I’m deep in the Oxygen builder, structuring dynamic data with ACF and CPT UI, or optimizing PayPal gateways for a smooth checkout, my goal is always to bridge the gap between robust functionality and a seamless user experience. When I’m not refining site speed, I’m exploring the world of 3D design and finding new ways to push the boundaries of modern web development.
